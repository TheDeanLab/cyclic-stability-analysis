# Golgi segmentation revision, 24 September 2026

The old low-tail SBR threshold admitted extensive cytoplasmic background. The diagnostic projected a union of nine masks, making it even more permissive than any one measured mask. In Cell2 round 2, the middle old mask contained 12,672,070 voxels; the first ordinary Otsu pilot contained 108,731. The problem was excessive foreground, despite the bright visible Golgi.

## Prespecified method

Use `config/analysis-golgi-otsu.yaml`. For each target volume, subtract the median eligible exterior-ROI intensity separately in each Z plane. Apply normalized Gaussian smoothing with sigma **0.1083 expanded µm** (1 lateral voxel and 0.361 axial voxels), using only eligible ROI voxels. Remove the remaining per-plane median shift, record the resulting background, and clip negative values to zero. Compute one Otsu threshold over this corrected segmentation-image histogram, including zeros, using 1 intensity-unit bins. Retain 3D voxels strictly above the threshold, then retain 26-connected components with volume at least **0.1 expanded µm³**. With voxel spacing 0.1083 × 0.1083 × 0.3 µm, this means **29 voxels**. There is no closing, filling, or largest-object selection. The same physical smoothing is used for native and registered mask construction; measured intensities are never smoothed. Otsu follows the [scikit-image implementation](https://scikit-image.org/docs/stable/api/skimage.filters.html#skimage.filters.threshold_otsu).

A nonconstant volume fails signal admission if its background noise scale is zero/nonfinite or its Otsu threshold is below three robust background noise scales. The segmentation noise scale is 1.4826 times the median absolute deviation of the processed values from their individual plane medians over eligible voxels. The raw background-noise scale is saved separately and used for raw signal-to-background reporting. This prevents pure noise from becoming a large connected measured structure; it does not raise or otherwise modify the Otsu threshold. Constant background gives an explicitly empty mask. A histogram range above 1,000,000 intensity units fails rather than allocating an unbounded histogram. These safeguards do not establish specificity for Golgi.

Native signal segmentation uses a target-independent exterior ROI within 18 expanded µm of the complete native DAPI foreground, without nuclear component-count or morphology-quality restrictions. Registered morphology uses the existing common registration-valid support within 18 expanded µm of accepted nuclei, excluding the complete DAPI foreground. This changes the native Otsu signal domain from the former full extranuclear field; old and new absolute integrated values must not be interpreted as a biological change. Both use the same preprocessing, Otsu and component rules, with independently estimated thresholds/backgrounds in their respective domains. Normalized convolution prevents excluded nuclear or distant signal from bleeding into the threshold image. The calculation crops internally to the exact ROI bounds and restores all saved masks to their original geometry. Original, unsmoothed intensities minus the recorded plane background are integrated and divided by target exposure; exposure comparisons remain unchanged.

## DAPI and nuclear exclusion

The nuclear pipeline subtracts each DAPI plane's 10th percentile before positive 99th-percentile scaling. It then uses the existing consensus foreground and Lamin-style physical distance watershed with 2 µm seed prominence and 26-connectivity. Component quality criteria are unchanged. The five-field pilot accepted 3, 1, 2, 1, and 2 nuclei, respectively; Cell1 and Cell3 previously had merged nuclei and excess basal foreground.

Golgi masks exclude the **complete DAPI foreground**, including components rejected from nuclear morphology measurement. This is a voxelwise 3D exclusion. A Golgi maximum projection can overlap a projected nuclear outline because those foreground voxels lie at different Z positions. Same-plane panels and saved overlap counts provide the relevant exclusion check.

## Evidence and parameter choice

`results/golgi-otsu-component-study-20260923/` contains reproducible scripts, input provenance, two 675-condition sweeps, and five-field visual comparisons. Each sweep covers 25 volumes, threshold factors 0.8/1/1.2, and nine component cutoffs. Plain Otsu failed on Cell4/5 round 1 by selecting the acquisition background offset. Plane-median subtraction corrected that failure. The fixed 0.1 µm³ cutoff removed less than 0.5% of foreground volume in most rounds 2–5 and 2.7–4.7% in round 1. Larger cutoffs substantially removed visible signal; the smallest practical cutoff was selected without optimizing any between-round biological trend.

Exploratory sweeps reused the previous saved exterior ROI to isolate the threshold comparison. Production rebuilds the DAPI mask, ROI and registration validity from admitted artifacts. The final minimal-smoothing revision is additionally checked in `native-final-pilot/` and `registered-final-pilot/`. Therefore production values need not equal the study values exactly. The DAPI pilot and full-production smoke are separate validation stages.

## Full-workflow smoke and native-image correction

The first full Cell2 run, `results/20260924-golgi-otsu-v3`, completed registered segmentation with visually appropriate masks and zero nuclear overlap. Its native round-1 stage was correctly rejected by the contrast gate (Otsu threshold 5; raw noise scale 5.93). Restricting the native domain alone still gave threshold 6. Minimal smoothing over the full native field was also unstable: an outlying peak of 7,540 counts, compared with 887 near the nucleus, drove one threshold to 921.

The fixed 18 µm DAPI-defined native ROI plus sigma 0.1083 µm normalized Gaussian gave threshold 39, processed noise scale 1.887, and 94,729 retained voxels in the bounded pilot. Sigma 0.16245 µm increased the retained volume, so the smaller successful smoothing was selected. The failed full attempt is preserved; no successful terminal record was fabricated. The background-contrast gate remains unchanged. The production retry is in a new result root and applies the same physical smoothing to registered and native masks.

## Outputs and interpretation

Each registered diagnostic archive contains one retained mask per round, removed small components, per-plane backgrounds, accepted nuclear labels, and the full nuclear exclusion mask. `golgi-segmentation-qc.json` records thresholds, size counts, background noise, and overlap counts. Each native round has a separate mask archive and JSON recording the exact backgrounds used for integrated signal. Diagnostic panels show raw signal, retained foreground, removed components, and a same-Z overlay rather than a union of thresholds.

Legacy endpoint names ending in `_median` are retained for table compatibility: morphology summaries now summarize **one prespecified mask**. Gradient similarity still summarizes its original three smoothing scales, and surface-agreement endpoints still use their configured tolerances. The one-mask `golgi-threshold-robustness.csv` is an endpoint export, not an uncertainty estimate. Full parameter sensitivity is in the separate study.

Puncta removed by size are not proven background; some may be genuine small Golgi fragments. Mask volume remains threshold-sensitive: changing the corrected Otsu threshold by ±20% changes measured volume appreciably. These outputs quantify technical image stability, not biological replication or demonstrated organelle specificity. Review the preserved removed components, nuclear inventories, and image support alongside summary plots.
